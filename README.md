## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Main features:
Direct farmer-to-consumer selling.
FPO and bulk buyer connections.
Real-time agricultural market price updates.
AI-based demand forecasting.
Smart logistics and route optimization.
Online ordering and payment.
Multilingual support for rural farmers.
Product quality and farmer verification.
How it addresses the problem:
By reducing unnecessary intermediaries, farmers can receive better prices for their products, while consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products at more affordable prices.
Innovation and uniqueness:
Our platform combines direct agricultural trading, AI-based demand prediction, and intelligent logistics in one system. It also supports regional languages, making it accessible to farmers in rural areas.
## Technical Approach
Component        Technology
Frontend         HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap
Backend          Python Django
Database         MySQL
AI/ML            Python, Scikit-learn
Maps             Google Maps API
Authentication   OTP-based verification
Payment          UPI integration

Implementation methodology:
1.Farmer registers and creates a profile.
2.Farmer uploads available products, quantity, and price.
3.Buyers search for nearby agricultural products.
4.AI predicts demand and recommends suitable prices.
5.Buyers place orders directly with farmers.
6.The logistics module identifies suitable delivery routes.
7.Payments are processed digitally.
8.Products are delivered to consumers or bulk buyers.
## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility:
1.The platform can be developed using existing web technologies.
2.Farmers can access it through smartphones.
3.Cloud hosting can support multiple users.
4.AI models can be developed using available agricultural market data.

Potential challenges:
  Challenge                         Solution
1.Lack of digital knowledge         Simple interface and Tamil support
2.Transportation problems           Local delivery partners
3.Price fluctuations                AI-based price suggestions
4.Product quality issues            Farmer and product verification
5.Internet connectivity             Lightweight, mobile-friendly platform

Viability:
The platform can generate revenue through a small service fee on completed transactions, logistics partnerships, and optional services for bulk buyers.
